## Open Federation Explorer

The **Federation Explorer** is the discovery surface for MCP areas published to the FrootAI Marketplace.

### What you'll see

- **Catalog tab** — every published area with its trust tier (first-party-ms · verified-publisher · community), install count, and publisher
- **Attached tab** — areas already attached to your running kernel, with idle timer + tool count

### Try it now

Press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+F12</kbd> (Win/Linux) or <kbd>Cmd+Shift+F12</kbd> (Mac) — or click the action button on this step — to open the explorer.

> Tip: the Federation Explorer is also available from the FrootAI sidebar under **MCP Areas → Discover**.
